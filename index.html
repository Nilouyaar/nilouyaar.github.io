A little about me

My story began long before the data.

I grew up in Iran, where my interest in the environment was shaped by the relationship between communities, natural resources, and the places they call home.

My early work involved community-based environmental research and practical questions about water access and sustainability. I later moved to the United States to pursue graduate research in environmental governance and public participation at North Carolina State University.

Today, as a policy analyst at Frontier Group in Boston, I bring those perspectives together. I use Python, GIS, and interdisciplinary research to investigate environmental problems, communicate what the evidence shows, and help make complex findings meaningful to a wider audience.
